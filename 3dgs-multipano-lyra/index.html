MultiPano → Lyra 2.0 (video-diffusion prior) · One Codex-generated panorama of the chapel + a custom 161-frame trajectory through pins 1→2→3. Lyra's video diffusion hallucinates a 3D-consistent flythrough video (~80 s), then its feed-forward DA3+VIPE reconstructor turns the video into a 11M-gaussian splat scene (subsampled here to 1.2M for the browser). The whole thing is generated from a single image — by construction the views are 3D-consistent, which is what the naive-multi-view version failed at.
Caveat: Lyra's gaussians are pixel-aligned (each frame contributes view-aligned shells), so the scene renders cleanly along the original trajectory — see the two videos top-right — but you'll see ghosting if you fly the camera off-trajectory.
